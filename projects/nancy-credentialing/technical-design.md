# P-004 — Technical design

Status: **Research prototype only / no sensitive production data**

## Safe initial system

The first version should be a requirements and progress tracker using public payer requirements and synthetic applicant data. It may show:

- requirement name and authoritative source;
- applicability and dependency;
- status, owner, due date, and evidence requested;
- review history and unresolved questions.

## Excluded until separately approved

- Social Security numbers, dates of birth, licenses, NPIs, tax identifiers, banking details, patient records, payer credentials, or identity documents;
- autonomous form submission;
- payer portal credentials in student-controlled systems;
- clinical documentation, coding, claims, or care recommendations;
- representations that the software provides legal or credentialing advice.

## Required ownership

A qualified adult must own data protection, payer credentials, regulatory interpretation, and submission approval. Complete the [technical-design template](../_templates/technical-design.md) before expanding scope.
