# Open decisions

These are the decisions most likely to change the program’s shape. They should be resolved explicitly rather than buried in implementation.

## Identity and governance

- Is “University of College” the public name, a working name, or a program inside another organization?
- Who owns the entity, client contracts, code, shared infrastructure, and reusable modules?
- Who is the accountable adult for students, production systems, and client complaints?
- What does a client own when bespoke work becomes a reusable product?

## Business model

- Are initial projects free, sponsored, fixed-fee, subscription, or revenue share?
- Are students paid, receiving stipends, earning credit, or participating through a camp/program fee?
- What service level is promised after launch?
- When does a referral-based consultancy become a vertical product company?

## Student safeguards

- What ages are eligible?
- Which communication channels permit adult/minor interaction?
- What consent is required for recording, transcription, photographs, and public demos?
- Which categories of data and production actions are prohibited for students?

## Technical architecture

- What is the approved shared application stack?
- Where do organization data, identities, audit logs, secrets, and deployment records live?
- What requires human approval in the communication-to-code pipeline?
- How are client isolation, backup, rollback, and offboarding tested?

## Program capacity

- What evidence must a student show before moving from one client to several?
- What is the maximum supported client load per student?
- How many students can one senior engineer supervise safely?
- Who covers support during school, travel, illness, or student turnover?

## “Home Schooled”

- Is the guest physically in their own home, or is the church/Longtail set the consistent “home”?
- Who are the hosts and what personal perspective do they bring?
- Is 20–30 minutes the total target, or should both proposed 10–15 minute halves be preserved rigidly?
- How closely should the show carry UofC branding?
- Which prospective guest names and affiliations need correction?
- What release, recording, and youth-participation rules apply?

## Strategic scope

- Is the independent-primary-care venture a UofC client vertical, a co-located partner, or a separate company?
- Should any healthcare project enter the student program before dedicated clinical, legal, privacy, and security leadership exists?
- Is Berea the permanent home, the first pilot community, or one node in a national model?
